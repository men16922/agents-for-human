## Title

Rehearsal — Test Purchasing Agents with Amazon Nova 2 Lite | Agents for Humans

## Description

A successful payment is not a delivered order. Rehearsal helps developers and QA engineers test purchasing agents under controlled failures and verify what actually happened.

This demo uses Amazon Nova 2 Lite on Amazon Bedrock, Strands Agents SDK, an independent local Medusa commerce backend, and an English read-only dashboard. The continuous recording shows an actual Nova run under a supplier stock change: three tents and six lights delivered for 380 synthetic credits, verified against independent transaction records.

The video also includes a separate failed run and a frozen comparison across four methods. Failures and model costs remain in the results. These experiments do not establish a general Peer Review advantage or production readiness.

## Title

Rehearsal — Test Purchasing Agents on AWS | Nova 2 Lite + AgentCore

## Description

A payment is not a delivery. Rehearsal gives developers and QA teams a controlled place to test purchasing agents and verify what actually happened.

Try the English AWS demo: https://d1u9yhii3gor6j.cloudfront.net

This video shows Amazon Nova 2 Lite using Strands on AgentCore, with an independent Lambda/DynamoDB commerce backend and a Step Functions seller. The continuous, original-speed AWS recording delivers three tents and six lights for 380 synthetic credits after supplier A loses its tents. The verifier independently checks the journal; the runtime session is then stopped.

Choose fixed rules, direct Nova, isolated practice, or practice with peer review. Shared usage accounting includes learning, review and purchasing. The historical 80-cell comparison shown later is separate from the AWS deployment checks and does not establish a Peer Review advantage.
